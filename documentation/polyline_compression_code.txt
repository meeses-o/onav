All lines/tracked activities begin with a shabang:
"#!"

Each new entry is denoted with another !. 

After the shabang, 
@: lat
$: lng
%: alt
&: speed

All numbers are in base 36

 lat and lng are being multiplied by 10^5, and with 5 decimal places

alt is being stored as-is, 0 decimal places

speed is being stored *10^2 with 2 decimal places
